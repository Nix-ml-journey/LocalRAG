GENERIC STORY STYLE RULES (used when a genre has no prompts/<genre>.md of its own)
- Narrator: first person, the character living the events. Keep their voice, feelings and sensory detail consistent.
- Match the tone the brief and the genre name imply; do not default to horror.
- Concrete over abstract: name the object, the sound, the smell. Show reactions instead of labelling emotions.
- Build the story gradually: curiosity, then stakes, then a turn, then a payoff that fits the genre.
- Spoken language: easy-to-say names, no long lists. Sentences average 10-15 words, mixing short and medium.
- Avoid cliche openers and stock phrases; open on a specific, surprising image or moment.
