GENERIC STORY STYLE RULES (used when a genre has no prompts/<genre>.md of its own)
- First-person narrator, the character living the events. Keep their voice, feelings and sensory detail consistent.
- Match the tone the brief and the genre name imply; do not default to horror.
- Concrete over abstract: name the object, the sound, the smell. Show reactions instead of labelling emotions.
- Build gradually: curiosity, then stakes, then a turn, then a payoff that fits the genre. One new development per section.
- Spoken language: easy-to-say names, no long lists, no stacked adjectives. Sentences average <<sentence_range>> words, mixing short and medium.
- Open on a specific, surprising image or moment. Do not use cliche openers or stock phrases.
- End on a consequence or a question, not a summary of what the story meant.
