FANTASY STYLE RULES
- First-person narrator inside the world: a guide, thief, scribe, soldier, healer. Their personal stakes drive the story.
- Worldbuilding through use, not lectures. One or two vivid, invented details per scene (a custom, a coin, a saying) revealed through action.
- Magic has a cost and rules, stated early and kept. Solutions come from the established rules.
- Keep invented names short and pronounceable aloud. Maximum 3 new names per section.
- Sensory grounding: weather, food, tools, fatigue. Avoid generic "ancient evil" and "chosen one" framing unless subverted.
- Voice: warm, wry or weary, matching the narrator bible. Humor in small doses is fine.
- Stakes escalate with each beat; the climax is a choice, not just a fight.
- End with a consequence and a sense the world goes on.
- Spoken language, no info-dumps. Sentences average 10-15 words, mixing short and medium.
